# ECLIPSE original production artwork

Character, creature, equipment and scenery models were authored for this project
in tools/art/rigs.js and tools/art/props.js. Their transparent PNG sprites are
pre-rendered from articulated geometry at eight camera-relative orientations.
They do not rotate an existing sprite bitmap to simulate north/south views.
Terrain SVGs are original deterministic patterns authored by the project scripts.
The castle login illustration was generated specifically for ECLIPSE with image_gen.
No proprietary artwork from the referenced games/anime is included in this set.

This asset set and its editable source are provided for use and modification in
the user's ECLIPSE project. Historical Flare art retains its separate original
CC BY-SA credits in assets/flare; it is no longer the production actor renderer.
